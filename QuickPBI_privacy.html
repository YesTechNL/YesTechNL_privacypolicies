QuickPBI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

QuickPBI is a browser extension by YesTechNL that creates Azure DevOps work items (Features and Product Backlog Items) from text you select.

What is stored on your device

The settings you enter (Azure DevOps organization, project, Personal Access Token, area path, iteration, defaults) and any draft you have not saved are stored in the extension's local storage in your browser profile. They are not synced to other devices and are not encrypted. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its data removes them.

What is sent, and where

When you create a work item or use the side pane, QuickPBI sends the title, description and page link you entered, together with your Personal Access Token, to Azure DevOps (dev.azure.com) so the work item can be created and your project's area path and iteration lists can be loaded. This happens only when you use the extension.

What we do not do

YesTechNL does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. QuickPBI has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no servers of its own. The extension reads page content only for the text you select when you choose a menu entry, and it reads the clipboard only when you press Paste.

Your control

Use a token with the smallest scope (Work Items, Read & write) and a short expiry. You can revoke it at any time in Azure DevOps under User settings, Personal access tokens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
